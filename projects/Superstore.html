Superstore


A classic dataset found on Kagglehub. This analysis aims to provide practice on data visualisation and business analysis.


Research Question

How can the company maximise profits?


Initial insights into the data shows us that Superstore has had increasing revenue over the years. It's main revenue comes from sales in the Technology category, with highest sales in California, New York, and Washington. However, Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Illinois have generated negative profits.


States

California

Major cities like California and San Francisco lead the chage in sales and profits. This is expected as both states are major hubs for IT development.
