Superstore


A classic dataset found on Kagglehub. This analysis aims to provide practice on data visualisation and business analysis.


Research Question

How can the company maximise profits?


Initial insights into the data shows us that Superstore has had increasing revenue over the years. It's main revenue comes from sales in the Technology category, with highest profits in California, New York, and Washington. However, Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Illinois have generated negative profits.
The Furniture category has moderate sales, but generate the least profits. This implies that the category is used as a Loss Leader, with Tables being the top example.
Sub-Categories that generated the highest profit-margins are from: Paper, Copiers, Binders, Envelopes and Labels.

Technology Category

Phones lead the Technology category in the most sales, while Copiers lead in profits. Machines has the least profit, but it is important to note that it contains the highest average discount: 30.84%. The profits have been steadily increasing over the years, even when there was decreasing sales in 2024 compared to 2023. This category should be heavily invested in since it generates the most ROI. Perhaps more marketing needs to be done for the Machine sub-category so that there is no need to sell products at a discount. The heavy discounted machines came from the states Colorado and Ohio.


Office Supplies Category

Office Supplies as a category has also been increasing in sales and profits over the years, including a decrease in sales in 2024 compared to 2023. Although the Binders sub-cateogry has generated positive profits, it is important to note that in the states that generated negative profits (Texas, Illinois, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Florida), Binders were sold at 70-80% discount, often having a negative profit margin of ~160%. The sales of binders in these states need to be investigated. Binders were sold more in 2026 than 2025, but the profits decreased.


Furniture Category

The sales of Furniture has steadily increased from 2023 to 2026, but the profits have remained stable. Tables and Bookcases have generated negative profits. The profits have been dragged down by states that have generated negative profits, notably: Texas, Illinois, and Pennsylvania, where no sub-category has been positive. Tables throughout the years lead as the fourth highest seller amongst all sub-categories, but have generated negative profits, including in states that are positive. The relationship between chairs and tables should be investigated, it seems that tables have been sold at a high discount of 25%.


Notable Findings

There are a few states where sales have been increasing over the years, but profits have decreased: Texas, Illinois, Pennsylvania, North Carolina, Colorado, Aizona and Tennessee. Ohio also follows this pattern, but a surge in phone sales saved it in 2026.
Chairs and Phones lead in the highest sales overall, with phones generating more profits.
Although Envelopes and Labels have minimal sales, they generate high profit margins, 42.28% and 43.90% respectively.

Insights

1. Promotions should be done for Envelopes and Labels to genreate more sales. 2. Tehcnology category produce the most sales and profitability. Focus should be put on marketing, promotions and bundles. 2. Despite generating lower sales than Chairs, Tables produce negative profits. If customers purchasing Tables also buy high-margin products, the losses may be justified. Otherwise, pricing, discounting, or cost structures should be reviewed to improve profitability. 3. States that are underperforming should be explored. Are there local competitors in the area? Is it worth having a store in those areas? Can the funds be distributed to increase sales in other states?
