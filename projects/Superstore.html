Superstore


A classic dataset found on Kagglehub. This analysis aims to provide practice on data visualisation and business analysis.


Research Question

How can the company maximise profits?


Initial insights into the data shows us that Superstore has had increasing revenue over the years. It's main revenue comes from sales in the Technology category, with highest profits in California, New York, and Washington. However, Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Illinois have generated negative profits.
Sub-Categories that generated the highest profit-margins are from: Paper, Copiers, Binders and Accessories.
The Furniture category has moderate sales, but generate the least profits. This implies that the category is used as a Loss Leader, with Tables being the top product.


States

Positive Profit

Major cities like Los Angeles and San Francisco lead the charge in sales and profits. This is expected as both states are major hubs for IT development. Although most sales come from the Technology category, most of the profits is from Office Supplies. The same case for New York, as it serves as a big office hub for business. Most profits are from Office Supplies and Technology. Washington as a state have slightly different statistics. The highest Sub-Category in sales come from the loss leader identified earlier, Tables.
The sale of Office Supplies and Technology is what the company should focus on to generate the most profits. In leading states (California, New York, and Washington) focus should be put on these two categories.


Negative Profit

Texas is the state with the lowest profit. Upon further investigation, it has happened through all 4 years, with the highest loss in Office Supplies and Furniture. North Carolina is one of the only states that generated a negative profit in the Technology category. The reasons behind this is due to heavily discounted items (50-80%) in Copiers, Machines and Binders.


Sub-Categories

I investigated the top 5 most sold Sub-Categories in 2026: Phones, Chairs, Binders, Storage, abd Copiers.


Phones

Phones are one of the top products sold. Only in Ohio and Pennsylvania did phones generate a negative profit.
